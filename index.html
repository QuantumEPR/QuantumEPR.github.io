Research

I’m broadly interested in 3D learning, generation, and reconstruction—how AI systems can perceive and model the physical world in three dimensions. My past work has focused on inferring geometry, motion, and appearance from images through radiance-field-based representations. I’m also expanding my interests toward robot learning, exploring how embodied agents can leverage 3D understanding to perceive and interact with their environments.
