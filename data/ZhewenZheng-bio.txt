Zhewen Zheng is pursuing an M.S. in Computer Vision at Carnegie Mellon University. He holds dual Bachelor’s degrees in Mathematics and Informatics from the University of Washington, where he graduated with Interdisciplinary Honors. His research interests include 3D learning, visual reconstruction, and robot learning. He previously worked at ObvioHealth as a Machine Learning Engineer and at Shanghai AI Laboratory on radiance-field-based 3D reconstruction.
