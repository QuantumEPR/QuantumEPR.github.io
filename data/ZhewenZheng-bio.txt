Zhewen Zheng is an M.S. in Computer Vision candidate at Carnegie Mellon University, expecting to graduate in December 2026. He is interested in how intelligent systems perceive their surroundings, anticipate how the world changes, and use that understanding to guide action. His work brings together 3D vision and physical modeling to study dynamic scenes and human-object interaction. At CMU, he works on PhysON with Dr. Yizhou Zhao and Prof. Laszlo Jeni, and on Clay4D with Prof. Kris Kitani. He is a co-author of ARTDECO (ICLR 2026), developed during his time as a Research Assistant at Shanghai AI Laboratory. Previously, he was a Machine Learning Engineer at ObvioHealth. He holds a B.S. in Informatics and a B.A. in Mathematics from the University of Washington, where he graduated with Interdisciplinary Honors.
